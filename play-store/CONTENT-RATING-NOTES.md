# AM STUDIO — IARC Content Rating Notes

Do not guess or manually advertise a final age rating before completing the Google Play/IARC questionnaire. The rating must reflect every type of content accessible in the submitted Play build.

## Catalog themes that may affect the questionnaire
- Action and fantasy combat.
- Horror / frightening imagery in series such as 13 Pintu Neraka.
- Criminal/conflict themes in some universes.
- Gambling/card/casino themes in The Legendary Decks / related titles where applicable.
- Satire and potentially mature social themes.
- Hikayat Pohon Ganja includes historical/scientific/educational discussion related to cannabis; it must not be marketed as promoting illegal drug use.

## Submission rules
- Answer the IARC questionnaire from the actual public catalog available in the submitted AAB.
- Do not base the answer on internal drafts or hidden Admin content that users cannot access.
- If a newly released series introduces stronger violence, sexual content, drug-use depiction, gambling, strong language, or other rating-relevant content, update the content-rating questionnaire before/with the relevant app release when required.
- Store icon, screenshots, and feature graphic should remain suitable promotional metadata even if the app receives a mature content rating.
- `public/play-release.json` must remain closed while the final content-rating review is incomplete.
